What is Solitaire?

Solitaire, also known as Klondike Solitaire, is a popular browser game that challenges players to arrange cards in ascending order from the tableau and foundation piles.

Game Overview

Solitaire typically consists of a 52-card deck, with seven columns of nine cards each. The objective is to move all cards to the foundation piles, which are built up in suit from Ace to King.
